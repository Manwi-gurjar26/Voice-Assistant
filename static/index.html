How it works

Four stages, none of them waiting on you

01

Knows when you stop

Voice-activity detection runs turn by turn, so Aura starts thinking the instant you finish — not a beat later.

02

Hears you properly

Deepgram's nova-3 model streams your speech to text as you talk, punctuation and all, even over background noise.

03

Replies like a person

Short, spoken-style answers — one idea at a time, never a wall of text read out loud at you.

04

Can be interrupted

Talk over her and she stops, listens, and picks the conversation back up. The way a real one works.

Privacy

Where your voice actually goes

Mic only when you say so

Your microphone stays off until you press the button. Nothing is captured before that.

Streamed, not stored

Audio travels over an encrypted LiveKit connection straight to Deepgram for transcription — it isn't saved as a file anywhere.

Only the transcript reasons

The language model sees the text of what's said, not the raw audio, to work out a reply.

You're in control

Clear wipes the visible history any time, and closing the tab ends the session completely.
